Data Deletion

Last updated: 22 September 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you. This page explains how, and what happens next.

How to request deletion

  1. Email mtehreeem@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request.
  2. Tell us the email address on your account, or the order number if you checked out as a guest. This is how we find your records.
  3. If you contacted us on WhatsApp and want those messages deleted, include the phone number you messaged us from.

What we delete

  • Your account: name, email address and phone number.
  • Your delivery addresses and any saved wishlist or shopping bag.
  • WhatsApp message content, where you have asked for it.

What we may have to keep

We keep a record of completed orders — what was bought, the amount, and the payment reference — for as long as accounting and tax rules require. We keep no card details at all, so there are none to delete. Where we must keep an order record, we remove the personal contact details from it where we are able.

How long it takes

We will confirm your request by reply, and complete the deletion as quickly as we can. If we cannot delete something and have to keep it, we will tell you what and why.

Requests for information we do not hold
We can only delete data we actually hold. WhatsApp itself keeps its own records of your messages, which we cannot delete for you — you can manage that within WhatsApp.
