Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Maison Velour sells luxury beauty products online. This policy explains what we collect when you shop with us or message us on WhatsApp, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and phone number when you register.
  • Order details — the delivery name, phone number, address, city, country and postcode you give at checkout, along with what you ordered and what it cost.
  • Payment records — which method you used (card or cash on delivery) and whether the payment succeeded. We also store the payment provider's reference for the transaction.
  • Message content — if you contact us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number and the messages you send, so we can reply and look up your order.

Card details

We never see or store your card number, expiry date or security code. Card payments are taken on our payment provider's own secure page, and the card details go directly to them. We only receive the result: whether the payment succeeded, and a reference for it.

Why we use it

  • To take payment and deliver your order.
  • To answer your questions, including looking up the status of an order you ask about.
  • To keep records of transactions, as accounting and tax rules require.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of our service.

Who we share it with

  • Our payment provider (Safepay) — to take card payments and process refunds.
  • Our delivery partners — the name, address and phone number needed to deliver your order.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — messages you send us are delivered through WhatsApp, and are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.
  • Our hosting and database providers — who store the site and its data on our behalf.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept while your account is open and afterwards for as long as accounting rules require. WhatsApp conversations are kept only as long as they are useful for supporting you.

Your choices

  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal data, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you on WhatsApp.

See Data Deletion for how to request deletion, or contact us using the details below.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your shopping bag, your wishlist and your currency choice. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact us about privacy
Email mtehreeem@gmail.com and we will respond as quickly as we can. Please include your order number if your question is about an order.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
